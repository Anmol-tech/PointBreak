# Key Employee Agreements

## Retention Bonuses
Priya Raman and Marcus Webb each receive a retention bonus of $150,000 if they remain employed 12 months after a change of control.

## Acceleration
Unvested stock options of the four key employees accelerate fully if they are terminated without cause within 12 months after a change of control (double-trigger acceleration).

## Non-Compete
Key employees are bound by 12-month non-solicitation clauses. California law prevents enforcing non-compete clauses.
