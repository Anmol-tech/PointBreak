# Income Statement Fiscal Year 2025

## Summary
Falcon Analytics grew revenue 38% in fiscal 2025 and reached operating profitability in the fourth quarter.

## Quarterly Results
| Quarter | Revenue | Gross Profit | Operating Income |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | $2,450,000 | $1,690,000 | -$410,000 |
| Q2 | $2,780,000 | $1,950,000 | -$220,000 |
| Q3 | $3,050,000 | $2,170,000 | -$60,000 |
| Q4 | $3,320,000 | $2,420,000 | $180,000 |

## Margins
Gross margin improved from 69% in Q1 to 73% in Q4 as hosting costs moved to the committed CloudHost contract.
