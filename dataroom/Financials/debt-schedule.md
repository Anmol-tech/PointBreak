# Debt Schedule

## Change of Control Prepayment
The term loan must be repaid in full upon a change of control, together with a 2% prepayment premium.

## Covenants
Falcon must keep at least $2,000,000 of unrestricted cash and maintain a minimum ARR of $9,000,000, tested quarterly.
