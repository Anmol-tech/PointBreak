# Northwind Logistics Master Subscription Agreement

## Parties and Term
Falcon Analytics, Inc. provides its route-optimization platform to Northwind Logistics LLC under a three-year master subscription agreement effective March 1, 2024 and ending February 28, 2027. The agreement renews automatically for one-year periods unless either party gives 90 days' notice.

## Fees
Northwind pays an annual subscription fee of $1,850,000, invoiced quarterly in advance. Fees may increase by no more than 5% per renewal year.

## Change of Control
If Falcon Analytics undergoes a change of control, including a merger or the sale of more than 50% of its voting shares, Northwind may terminate this agreement on 60 days' written notice without penalty. Falcon must notify Northwind within 10 business days of signing any change-of-control transaction.

## Data Protection
Falcon processes Northwind shipment data only to provide the service and must delete it within 30 days after termination.

## Liability
Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the prior 12 months, except for breaches of confidentiality.
