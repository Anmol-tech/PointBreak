# Initech Software Services Agreement

## Parties and Term
Falcon Analytics, Inc. provides analytics dashboards to Initech, Inc. on a month-to-month basis that began January 15, 2025.

## Fees
Initech pays $42,000 per month. Either party may end the agreement with 30 days' notice.

## Assignment
Falcon may assign this agreement to an affiliate or to a successor in a merger without Initech's consent. The agreement contains no change-of-control termination right.

## Support
Support is provided during business hours, with a four-hour response target for critical issues.
