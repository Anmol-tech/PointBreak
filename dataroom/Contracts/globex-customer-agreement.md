# Globex Retail Enterprise License Agreement

## Parties and Term
Falcon Analytics, Inc. licenses its demand-forecasting module to Globex Retail Corp. for five years starting July 1, 2023.

## Fees
Globex pays $2,400,000 per year plus usage fees above 40 million forecast calls per month.

## Change of Control
A change of control of Falcon Analytics requires Globex's prior written consent. If Globex does not consent, Globex may terminate the license and receive a pro-rated refund of prepaid fees. Assignment to an acquirer that competes with Globex is prohibited.

## Exclusivity
Falcon may not license the demand-forecasting module to any of the five named Globex competitors listed in Schedule C during the term.

## Service Levels
Falcon guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime, with service credits of up to 15% of monthly fees.
