# CloudHost Infrastructure Vendor Agreement

## Services
CloudHost Networks provides Falcon Analytics with managed compute and storage in two US regions under a committed-spend contract.

## Committed Spend
Falcon has committed to spend $3,600,000 over three years ending December 31, 2027. Unused commitment is payable as a shortfall fee.

## Termination and Change of Control
CloudHost may not terminate for a change of control of Falcon. Falcon may terminate for convenience by paying 50% of the remaining commitment.

## Security
CloudHost maintains SOC 2 Type II certification and must report security incidents within 24 hours.
