# Headquarters Office Lease

## Premises
Falcon Analytics leases 18,500 square feet at 400 Market Street, San Jose, California.

## Rent and Term
Base rent is $61,000 per month through June 30, 2029, with 3% annual increases.

## Assignment
The lease may be assigned to an acquirer of substantially all of Falcon's assets with the landlord's consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld.

## Security Deposit
Falcon has posted a security deposit of $366,000 in the form of a letter of credit.
